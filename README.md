## Project Overview

This portfolio demonstrates the practical use of generative AI to improve workplace productivity in the field of Health Informatics and Medical Documentation.

It presents reusable prompts and AI-assisted workflows for professional writing, information processing, planning, verification, responsible AI use, and future workplace integration.

The examples are designed around realistic workplace scenarios while avoiding the use of real confidential or sensitive information.

## Selected Workplace Scenario

The selected workplace scenario is the role of a Health Informatics Specialist working in Medical Documentation.

The portfolio focuses on practical activities such as preparing professional communications, organizing and summarizing workplace information, extracting action items, structuring information, supporting workflow planning, and reviewing AI-generated outputs before they are used in the workplace.

## Course Skills Demonstrated

- Prompt Engineering using the C.A.R.E. framework

- Professional Writing and communication workflows
- Information extraction, summarization, synthesis, and restructuring
- AI-assisted planning and workflow organization

## Key Learnings

1. A well-structured prompt produces more useful, consistent, and controllable AI outputs.
2. Generative AI can support workplace writing and information processing, but important facts and claims must be verified by a human.
3. AI outputs should be reviewed for accuracy, tone, completeness, and alignment with the original source.
4. Sensitive or confidential information should not be entered into AI tools unless the use is explicitly authorized and appropriately protected.
5. Human oversight and accountability remain essential when using AI in professional workplace activities.
