
# Responsible AI Use Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist explains how I will use Generative AI responsibly in my Health Informatics and Medical Documentation work. It focuses on privacy, safe workplace use, human accountability, accuracy, transparency, and responsible handling of information.

## 1. Privacy

I will not enter patient information, personal information, medical records, or other private data into Generative AI tools. I will use fictional, anonymized, or non-sensitive information whenever possible.

## 2. Confidentiality and Sensitive Information

I will protect confidential organizational information, internal reports, employee information, and sensitive workplace data. I will not upload confidential or restricted information to public or unauthorized AI tools.

## 3. Safe Workplace Use

I will use Generative AI for appropriate workplace tasks such as drafting, summarizing, organizing information, and planning. I will not use AI as the final decision-maker for sensitive professional or healthcare-related decisions.

## 4. Human Accountability

I remain responsible for the final output. AI-generated content must be reviewed by me before it is shared, submitted, or used in the workplace.

## 5. Accuracy and Verification

I will verify important facts, dates, numbers, and claims against the original source information. I will not assume that an AI-generated answer is correct simply because it is clearly written.

## 6. Transparency and Responsible Judgment

I will use professional judgment when using AI-generated content. When appropriate, I will disclose AI assistance and make sure that the final work reflects human review, judgment, and accountability.

## Information Classification: Green / Amber / Red

| Information Type | Classification | How I Will Handle It |
|---|---|---|
| General workplace writing instructions and fictional examples | Green | Safe to use with AI when no confidential information is included. |
| Anonymized workplace process information without names or sensitive details | Amber | Use only after removing identifying or confidential information and reviewing the output. |
| Patient information, medical records, personal identifiers, confidential reports, or sensitive organizational information | Red | Do not enter or upload to public or unauthorized AI tools. |

## Responsible Use Commitment

I will use Generative AI as a productivity assistant, not as a replacement for professional judgment. I will protect privacy and confidentiality, verify important information, and review all AI-generated content before using it.

This portfolio does not contain real patient information, confidential organizational information, or sensitive personal data. The examples are fictional or anonymized for training purposes.

## Key Learning

Responsible AI use requires a balance between productivity and professional responsibility. The goal is to gain the benefits of Generative AI while protecting information, verifying outputs, and maintaining human accountability.
