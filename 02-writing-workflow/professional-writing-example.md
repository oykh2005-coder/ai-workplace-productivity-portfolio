
# Professional Writing Example

## Workplace Scenario

The Medical Documentation Department needed to inform its employees that an upcoming department meeting had been rescheduled to September 25 so that all staff members would have the opportunity to attend.

## Writing Objective

The objective was to create a clear, concise, and professional email that communicated the new meeting date and encouraged all employees to attend.

## AI-Assisted Draft

**Subject: Meeting Rescheduled to September 25**

Dear Medical Documentation Team,

Please be informed that the upcoming meeting has been rescheduled to September 25 to ensure that all staff members have the opportunity to attend.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation. Please mark your calendar and ensure your attendance.

Your attendance and participation are highly valued.

Kind regards,  
Medical Documentation Department

## Why This Version Is Effective

The final email is appropriate for a professional workplace because it:

- Communicates the purpose immediately.
- Clearly states the new meeting date.
- Uses a formal and respectful tone.
- Includes a clear action for employees.
- Keeps the message concise and easy to understand.

## AI Review and Refinement

The AI-generated draft was reviewed before use. The message was refined to make the employee action clearer by adding a request to mark the calendar and ensure attendance.

The final content was checked for clarity, tone, relevance, and completeness.

## Key Learning

Generative AI can help create professional workplace communication quickly, but the user should review and refine the output to ensure that it accurately communicates the intended message and meets workplace communication standards.
