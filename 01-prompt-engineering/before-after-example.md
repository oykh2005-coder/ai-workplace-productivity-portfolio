
# Before-and-After Prompt Example

## Workplace Scenario

A Medical Documentation Department meeting needed to be rescheduled to September 25 so that all staff members would have the opportunity to attend.

## Before: Vague Prompt

> Write an email about the deadline change.

### Before Output

**Subject: Meeting Rescheduled to September 25**

Dear Medical Documentation Team,

Please be informed that the upcoming meeting has been rescheduled to September 25 to ensure that all staff members have the opportunity to attend.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation.

Kind regards,  
Medical Documentation Department

---

## After: Improved C.A.R.E. Prompt

> **Context:** I work in the Medical Documentation Department. The upcoming department meeting has been rescheduled to September 25 so that all staff members can attend.
>
> **Action:** Write a professional email informing the department employees about the new meeting date and asking them to attend.
>
> **Role:** Act as a professional workplace communication assistant.
>
> **Expected Output:** Write a concise and formal email in English. Include a clear subject line, the new meeting date, a request for employees to mark their calendars and attend, and a professional closing.

### After Output

**Subject: Meeting Rescheduled to September 25**

Dear Medical Documentation Team,

Please be informed that the upcoming meeting has been rescheduled to September 25 to ensure that all staff members have the opportunity to attend.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation. Please mark your calendar and ensure your attendance.

Your attendance and participation are highly valued.

Kind regards,  
Medical Documentation Department

---

## What Improved

The improved prompt produced a more useful workplace email because it provided clear context, a specific action, an appropriate professional role, and defined output requirements.

The main improvement was the addition of a clear employee action: **mark the calendar and ensure attendance**.

This example demonstrates how the C.A.R.E. framework can transform a vague prompt into a specific and reusable workplace prompt.

## Key Learning

A vague prompt may produce a general response. Adding context, action, role, and expected output makes the request more precise and helps generate a response that better matches the workplace need.
