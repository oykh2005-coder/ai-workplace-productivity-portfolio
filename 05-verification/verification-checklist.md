
# Verification Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist is used to verify AI-generated workplace content before it is used or shared. It is designed to support accuracy, consistency, and human accountability.

## Personal Verification Checklist

Before using an AI-generated workplace output, I check the following:

- [ ] **Facts:** Are all factual statements supported by the source information?
- [ ] **Dates and numbers:** Are dates, deadlines, numbers, and other specific details accurate?
- [ ] **Completeness:** Does the output include all important information from the original request or source?
- [ ] **No unsupported claims:** Has the AI introduced information, assumptions, names, or details that were not provided?
- [ ] **Consistency:** Does the final output remain consistent with the original information?
- [ ] **Clarity and tone:** Is the language clear, professional, and appropriate for the intended audience?
- [ ] **Human review:** Has a human reviewed the final output and taken responsibility for the information before use?

## Applied Verification Example

### Output Reviewed

The following AI-assisted email from the Professional Writing Workflow was reviewed:

**Subject: Meeting Rescheduled to September 25**

Dear Medical Documentation Team,

Please be informed that the upcoming meeting has been rescheduled to September 25 to ensure that all staff members have the opportunity to attend.

We appreciate your understanding and cooperation. Please mark your calendar and ensure your attendance.

Your attendance and participation are highly valued.

Kind regards,  
Medical Documentation Department

### Verification Results

| Check | Result | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| Facts | Pass | The email communicates only the meeting rescheduling information provided in the scenario. |
| Dates and numbers | Pass | The date is stated consistently as September 25. |
| Completeness | Pass | The email clearly communicates the new meeting date and attendance request. |
| No unsupported claims | Pass | No names, additional deadlines, or unsupported facts were added. |
| Consistency | Pass | The final email remains consistent with the original workplace scenario. |
| Clarity and tone | Pass | The language is concise, formal, and appropriate for workplace communication. |
| Human review | Pass | The output was reviewed and refined before being included in the portfolio. |

## Verification Result

The reviewed email passed all seven verification checks.

One refinement was made during the human review: the message was strengthened by adding a clear action for employees to mark their calendars and ensure attendance.

No real patient information, confidential organizational information, or sensitive data was used in this example.

## Key Learning

Verification should be part of the workflow, not an afterthought. AI-generated content should be checked against the original information, especially facts, dates, numbers, assumptions, and completeness.

The final decision to use an AI-generated output remains with the human reviewer.
